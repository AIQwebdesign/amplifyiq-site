- local storage;
- pixels;
- tags;
- scripts;
- tracking identifiers; and
- software-development or analytics technologies.

For convenience, this policy refers to these technologies collectively as "cookies".

#### Strictly Necessary Cookies

They may be used for purposes including:

- security;
- fraud prevention;
- network management;
- consent preferences;
- form functionality;
- session management; and
- technical website operation.

These cookies cannot normally be disabled through our consent tool where they are genuinely necessary to provide the website or a service requested by the user.

#### Functional Cookies

Functional cookies may provide enhanced features or remember choices made by a user.

Examples can include:

- interface preferences;
- language preferences;
- embedded third-party features; and
- enhanced website functionality.

Where consent is legally required for a functional technology, it will remain disabled until consent has been provided.

#### Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the website.

They may provide information concerning:

- visitor numbers;
- traffic sources;
- pages visited;
- time spent on pages;
- website interactions;
- device types; and
- website performance.
